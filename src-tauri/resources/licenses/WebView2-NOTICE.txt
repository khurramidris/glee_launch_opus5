Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime

Glee's Windows offline installer embeds Microsoft's redistributable Evergreen
Standalone Installer through Tauri's `offlineInstaller` bundle mode. The
WebView2 Runtime is installed and serviced separately by Microsoft.
