# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Glee should be a welcoming, professional project. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, patient with different experience levels, receptive to correction, and careful with other people's privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, sustained disruption, or retaliation against a good-faith reporter.

Technical disagreement is normal. Critique the idea and evidence, not the person. Maintainers may close repetitive, off-topic, abusive, or unsafe interactions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other spaces where someone is representing the Glee project.

## Reporting

Do not post a conduct report publicly if it contains private or identifying information. Contact a maintainer through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no project-private contact is available or a maintainer is involved, use GitHub's confidential abuse-reporting channel and retain relevant links or screenshots.

Reports should identify the behavior, location, approximate time, relevant context, and any immediate safety concern. Share only information needed to investigate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality as far as practical, hear affected parties where safe, and avoid conflicts of interest. Proportionate actions may include clarification, a warning, content removal, interaction limits, temporary suspension, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Good-faith reports will not be punished. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.
