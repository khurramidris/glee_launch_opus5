# Privacy

Glee is built so your conversations never leave your computer.

## What stays on your computer

- Characters, personas, chats, memories, and settings are stored in a local
  database in Glee's app data folder.
- Replies are generated by an AI model that runs on your own computer. Your
  messages are never sent to an AI service.
- Glee has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or advertising.

## What Glee downloads

The first time Glee starts, it downloads the AI files it needs: the chat model,
a small memory model, and the llama.cpp engine for your computer. These come
from the project's download mirror when one is configured, otherwise from
Hugging Face and GitHub. Like any download, these servers see your IP address
and the file being requested. Nothing about you or your chats is sent.

Every file is checked against a fingerprint (SHA-256) built into the app before
it is used.

## Update checks

Glee checks for new versions by fetching one small public file (`latest.json`)
from the project's GitHub releases about 20 seconds after it starts and then
after that no more than once every few hours. The request carries no account, device identifier, or
information about your chats; like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address
and that Glee (with its version number) asked for the file. When you choose to
download an update, the installer comes from GitHub and is checked against a
SHA-256 fingerprint before it is opened. This and the one-time AI download are
the only network traffic Glee creates. Turn automatic checks off under
**Settings → Updates**; you can still check by hand.

## Local network safety

The AI engine listens only on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`), requires a
secret key that changes every time Glee starts, and has its web interface and
slot-inspection endpoints turned off, so web pages and other programs cannot
read your conversations from it.

## Diagnostics

Diagnostics bundles are created only when you click **Export diagnostics**.
They stay on your computer until you choose to share them, and local folder
paths and your account name are replaced with placeholders.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Glee removes the app. To also remove your chats and the
downloaded AI files, delete Glee's app data folder (shown under
**Settings → Diagnostics**):

- Windows: `%APPDATA%\com.glee.desktop`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/com.glee.desktop`
